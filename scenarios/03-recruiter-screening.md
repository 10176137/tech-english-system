# HR 筛选与 90 秒技术自述 (Recruiter Screening)

> 欧洲招聘流程第 1 关：15~20 分钟语音电话。HR 只关注流利度、签证意向与技术栈契合度。

---

## 1. 90 秒技术自述 (Elevator Pitch - 4年 Java 专属)

> *"Hi Emma! Thanks for reaching out.*  
>  
> *I'm a backend software engineer with about **4 years of hands-on experience**, primarily focused on the **Java and Spring Boot ecosystem**.*  
>  
> *Currently, I’m working in the healthcare domain, where I own the core **order ingestion system** and the **workflow engine**. My main responsibility is managing the **state transitions** across the entire order lifecycle to ensure high reliability, data consistency, and idempotency.*  
>  
> *The reason I'm exploring opportunities in Europe is that I'm eager to step out of my comfort zone and work in a **diverse, international team** that values engineering autonomy and code quality. I'd love to bring my background in distributed systems to a global product."*

## 2. 签证与离职周期应对 (Visa & Logistics)

* **欧盟蓝卡签证赞助 (Visa Sponsorship)**：
  * *“I will need visa sponsorship for the EU Blue Card, but my university degree is already verified and recognized (Anabin H+).”*  
    （我需要欧盟蓝卡签证赞助，不过我的大学学位已完成认证，符合 Anabin H+ 资格。）
* **离职周期 (Notice Period)**：
  * *“My current notice period is around 30 days, so I can relocate or start once the visa is processed.”*  
    （我目前的离职交接期大约是 30 天，所以签证办好后我就可以随时入职或出国。）
